# Shine Academy — Admissions Enquiries via Google + Facebook Ads

Client Name: Shine Academy
Industry: education
Services Provided: Google Ads, Facebook/Meta Ads, Lead generation funnel optimization, Campaign tracking & optimization

## Overview

Google Ads + Facebook (Meta) Ads lead generation to increase admissions enquiries and improve conversion outcomes for Shine Academy.

## Challenge

- The academy had a poor experience with their previous marketing agency.
- They needed a reliable lead flow for admissions and better overall campaign performance.

## What we did

- **Google Ads:** High-intent search campaigns for admissions-related keywords, with location + course targeting.
- **Facebook/Meta Ads:** Lead campaigns and remarketing to capture and nurture interested students/parents.
- **Landing/lead flow optimization:** Better messaging, creative, and enquiry capture to improve lead quality.
- **Ongoing optimization:** Budget allocation, audience refinement, creative testing, and performance-based improvements.

## Results

- Significant increase in **admissions enquiries** through Google + Facebook campaigns.
- Client satisfaction improved after onboarding (compared to the previous agency experience).
- **~500–1000 admissions completed over the past months** (reported outcome).

## Notes / proof points

- Results depend on seasonality (admission windows), location, and ad budget; performance improved through continuous optimization and testing.
