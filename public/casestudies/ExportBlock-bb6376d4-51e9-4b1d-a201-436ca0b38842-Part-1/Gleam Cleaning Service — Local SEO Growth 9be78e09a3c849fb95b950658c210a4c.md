# Gleam Cleaning Service — Local SEO Growth

Client Name: Gleam Cleaning Service
Industry: service
Services Provided: Local SEO, Google Business Profile (GMB/GBP) optimization, Local listings/citations optimization, On-page SEO

## Overview

Local SEO + Google Business Profile optimization to increase calls, bookings, and daily pre-bookings for Gleam Cleaning Service.

## What we did

- **Local SEO strategy:** Targeted service + location keywords and local-intent pages.
- **Google Business Profile (GMB/GBP):** Proper optimization to improve map visibility and trust (categories, services, photos, posts, Q&A, reviews workflow).
- **Local listings / citations:** Consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) and directory cleanup to strengthen local ranking signals.
- **On-page SEO:** Title tags, headings, internal links, and conversion-focused landing pages.

## Results

- **Daily orders / pre-bookings increased from ~2/day to 20+ per day.**
- **Revenue increased by ~300%.**
- More inbound calls and booking requests driven by improved local visibility and optimization.

## Notes / proof points

- Growth came from improved local rankings, better map presence, and clearer conversion paths.
- Results can vary by location, competition, and seasonality; performance improved through ongoing optimization.
