# Tosh Clinic — Patient Walk-ins Growth

Client Name: Trichy Ortho and Skincare Health Foundation
Industry: healthcare
Services Provided: Social media marketing, Google Business Profile (GMB/GBP) management, Meta Ads, Google Ads

## Overview

Social media marketing + local presence optimization to increase patient walk-ins for Tosh’s clinic.

## What we did

- **Social Media Marketing (Meta):** Campaign strategy, creatives, audience targeting, and optimization focused on generating patient walk-ins.
- **Google Business Profile (GMB/GBP):** Profile optimization and ongoing management to improve local visibility and trust.
- **Google Ads:** Local search campaigns to capture high-intent patients looking for nearby clinic services.

## Results

- **Up to 200% growth in patient walk-ins** (combined impact from Meta + Google + improved local visibility).
- Increased trust and clinic credibility through consistent presence on Google and social platforms.
- More real patient walk-ins attributed to the campaigns and improved local discovery.

## Notes / proof points

- Patient walk-ins were confirmed through the clinic’s feedback and video proof shared.
- Results may vary by location, seasonality, and budget; campaigns were optimized based on performance signals.
